      Rules in short

      COOPERATE +1 Capability.
      RACE +2 Capability, −1 Safety.
      EXPOSE LEFT / RIGHT If that active neighbour chose RACE: you +2 Capability, their RACE is worth 1 less and they skip the next round. Otherwise nothing.
      RELEASE Save the points of your Capability level and leave this Generation. Safe from collapse.
      Safety +1 if a strict majority cooperates or exposes; −1 per RACE. Stays between 0 and 12.
      Collapse roll d12 after every round. If the roll is higher than Safety: everyone who has not released loses their Capability and the Generation ends.
      Points Capability 1–3 = 1 P · 4–6 = 2 P · 7–8 = 3 P · 9–10 = 4 P · 11–12 = 5 P.
      Generations 3 Generations of up to 6 rounds, starting Safety 12 / 11 / 10. After round 6 everyone still racing releases automatically. A last remaining player may race on alone.
